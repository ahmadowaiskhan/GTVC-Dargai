About Tevta

In 2002, Directorate of Technical Education and Manpower Training were merged to form the Directorate General of Technical Education & Manpower Training.
The future of Pakistan is linked with our capability of imparting quality and market-oriented skills to our youth for employability through technical education & vocational training.
