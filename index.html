Training Framework

Information About Training & Standards

Training at GTVC Dargai is structured according to national competency-based training standards to ensure every trainee graduates with real, hands-on workshop mastery.

Core Educational Principle

Practical Hands-On Training 80%
Theoretical & Concept Knowledge 20%
Training Duration: Standard duration of the vocational training courses is six (6) months of intensive learning.
Periodic Testing: Weekly and monthly trade tests are systematically conducted by trade experts of the Centre.
Workshop Uniform: All trainees must wear blue color overalls during workshop practical sessions for safety.
Skill Evaluation: Progress report for each trainee is meticulously recorded in two-month skill evaluation reports.
Attendance Threshold: Trainees must achieve at least 85% practical hours and 75% theoretical attendance to qualify for final trade examinations.
Leave Policy: Leaves are allowed only upon prior written approval of the Principal, without waiver of minimum attendance criteria.
