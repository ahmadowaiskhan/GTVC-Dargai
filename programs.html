High Demand Trade

Electrician Course CBT Level II & III

Electrician's work is physically demanding such as climbing ladders and lifting tools. This course teaches complete residential wiring, single and three-phase industrial installations, motor controls, solar PV wiring, and circuit testing.

Duration6 Months
AccreditationNAVTTC & KP-TEVTA
EligibilityMatric / Middle

Key Modules Covered:

  • Domestic & Industrial Wiring
  • Solar Panel & Inverter Setup
  • AC/DC Motors & Generators
  • Protective Relays & Breakers

Diploma in Information Technology (DIT)

Accredited by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Board of Technical Education (KPBTE) Peshawar. This 1-year diploma is mandatory for IT/Computer Operator government and private sector jobs.

Examination BodyKPBTE Peshawar
EligibilityMatric / Intermediate

Key Modules Covered:

  • Term 1: Office Automation, OS & C++

  • Networking & Hardware Troubleshooting
