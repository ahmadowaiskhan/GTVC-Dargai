Institutional History

Building Tomorrow's Skilled Workforce Today

The establishment of the Government Technical & Vocational Training Centre (GTVC) at Dargai, Malakand was realized to fulfill the outstanding demand of employers and industries across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Our core mandate is to produce high-tech, qualified, and disciplined technical workers who meet the rigorous standards of modern industry and represent Pakistan in global labor markets.

Recognized as a Model TEVT Institute (MI) and accredited by the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC), GTVC Dargai delivers modern Competency-Based Training (CBT) under the governance of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Technical Education and Vocational Training Authority (KP-TEVTA).

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier center of technical excellence in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, empowering youth with industry-aligned technical proficiencies, moral integrity, and entrepreneurial acumen to drive socioeconomic prosperity.

Our Mission

Impart market-driven 80% practical technical education using state-of-the-art machinery, competent instructors, and active industry linkages to ensure every graduate is readily employable locally and internationally.

Governance & Oversight

About KP-TEVTA

In 2002, the Directorate of Technical Education and Manpower Training were merged to establish the Directorate General of Technical Education & Manpower Training. Today, the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Technical Education and Vocational Training Authority (KP-TEVTA) stands as the autonomous body leading vocational reforms.

The future of Pakistan is inextricably linked with our capacity to impart quality, market-oriented vocational skills to youth. Under KP-TEVTA's strategic roadmap, GTVC Dargai receives modern training curricula, high-standard workshop equipment, and subsidized training funds.
